Remain in Plan. Inspect the exact decision version/hash, owner, rationale, alternatives and cited evidence. Use vault_search, vault_read and project_map_query for relevant missing context, respecting private/shared scope. Treat graph edges and old model text as claims until supported by current sources. Explain tradeoffs, cite concrete records and identify missing information. Never infer human approval or supersede a decision automatically. Offer a proposed revision for the owner to inspect through /decision. No shell, file write, publication or model delegation is authorized by this recipe.
